Antiphon keeps a playlist in step across Spotify and Apple Music. Pick a playlist on one side, and Antiphon finds or creates its twin on the other and stitches the two together into a seam.

SEE EVERY CHANGE FIRST
Every sync starts with a dry run. It shows exactly which tracks get added and where, and nothing is removed unless you say so.

ONE WAY OR BOTH WAYS
Copy one playlist into the other, or keep both in sync in both directions. Removals are never copied unless you turn that on, and Antiphon asks before removing anything in a two-way seam.

CLOSE CALLS ARE YOURS
When a match isn't certain, like a remaster or a live version, Antiphon asks instead of guessing. Play both versions and swipe to decide. You can also pick the exact version for any song.

THEN IT KEEPS WATCHING
Turn on watching and new tracks sync on their own. Activity shows what was added, and any sync can be undone.

FINDS THE PLAYLISTS YOU ALREADY HAVE
Antiphon spots playlists you keep on both sides and links them in one go.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Antiphon runs on your iPhone and iPad and talks to Spotify and Apple Music directly. There's no Antiphon server and no account. Your sign-ins stay in the iOS Keychain, and Antiphon only reads and edits playlists. It never plays, follows or posts anything.

WHAT YOU NEED
- Spotify Premium, and a Spotify developer client ID, which is free to create. Spotify only lets Premium accounts use the apps they create; Antiphon walks you through it in a few minutes.
- An Apple Music subscription, to add tracks to your Apple Music library and playlists.

Antiphon isn't affiliated with Spotify or Apple.
