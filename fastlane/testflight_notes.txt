What's new in 1.3.0:

• New design for iOS 26: Syncs, Library, Activity and Search on a Liquid Glass tab bar, and seams shown as two stitched covers.
• Every sync shows a preview first. Close matches wait in Review for your call, and removals follow each seam's rule (only add, mirror, or ask).
• Syncs keep running when you leave the app or lock the phone, with progress from iOS. Several seams can sync at once; tap the bar above the tabs to see them.
• Opening Antiphon checks every seam. Watched seams sync, and the others say what's waiting. Pull down on Syncs to check again right away.
• Activity lists every run, and most can be undone.
• Widgets: Seams, In sync (with Sync all), and New this week, plus a Live Activity while a sync runs.
• Answer questions from the notification: use a close match, skip it, or decide about a removal.
• Settings: monitoring interval, Wi-Fi only, quiet hours, removals in the background, appearance and app icon.

Focus for testers:
• Open the app after adding a track on Spotify: a watched seam should pick it up, and an unwatched seam's card should say "1 change waiting". Pull down to check again.
• Start a long sync, tap Hide, then switch apps or lock the phone. It should finish.
• Add the widgets and check that they match the app after syncing, linking or unlinking.
