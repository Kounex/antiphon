What's new in this build:

• Fixed: manually linking an unmatched track could add a duplicate to the playlist and show the song twice in the track list — matches now merge into the existing entry instead
• Fixed: the manual-match sheet could open blank and gave no feedback while linking — search now starts reliably and a "Linking track…" indicator shows progress
• Improved: unmatched tracks now say which side's copy failed (e.g. "Apple Music copy — Not found on Spotify"), so bidirectional sync results make sense

Focus for testers: flag a track as unmatched, link it manually to a song that already exists in the target playlist, and confirm no duplicate appears — in the app or on the platform.
