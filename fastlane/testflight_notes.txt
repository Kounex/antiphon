What's new in 1.5.0:

• Playlists you share with a friend can be stitched, also when Spotify doesn't mark them as collaborative. Playlists you only follow can't: Spotify doesn't give apps their tracks, and Antiphon says so when you pick one.
• Getting started: the first screen says what you need (Spotify Premium, an Apple Music subscription, two minutes for a Spotify key). Apple Music connects first. The client ID guide needs one trip to Spotify: "Open the dashboard" copies the redirect address for you.
• Settings › Spotify › Share this client ID sends a link that fills in your client ID on someone else's iPhone. Add their Spotify email under Users Management first; Spotify allows 5 people per client ID.
• Review: the three buttons say what they do (Skip, Other versions, Same track) and each card shows the album. A track you skip now shows as skipped in its seam, not as in sync.
• Matching: spelling the two catalogs disagree on (accents, "&" and "and", "Pt." and "Part") no longer makes a track "not found". A version by an artist whose name only contains the original's is asked about, never added on its own.
• Notifications name the new tracks instead of counting them.
• Settings › Monitoring shows how often iOS really ran Antiphon in the background, and how a Shortcuts automation can sync right after you close Spotify or Music.
• Problems: a playlist that was deleted can be made again from the other side. A playlist Spotify refuses is no longer reported as "Spotify signed you out".
• Fixed: picking another version of a song could take the song out of an Apple Music playlist when Apple Music was slow to show the new version.
• A seam's screen takes its colours from the cover. "Show who added each track" is gone: Spotify no longer tells apps who a collaborator is.

Focus for testers:
• Stitch a playlist a friend shares with you (New seam, Spotify side) and sync it. If tracks should go into the friend's playlist too, check that they arrive there: that direction is untested.
• Review a close match and tap Skip: the seam should list it under Skipped, and tapping it should let you choose a version after all.
• Pick another version of a synced song: the new one should take the old one's place, and the song should never be missing.
• A day after installing, look at Settings › Monitoring: it should say how often iOS ran Antiphon in the background.
• Rules of a one-way seam into an Apple Music playlist you made yourself: Track order should be a sentence, not three choices.

What's new in 1.4.5:

• Seam cards show what's happening. While a seam is checked, a gold ring spins round the eye between its covers; while it syncs, the arrows there turn. The card's border marks special states: gold with a light running round it while syncing or checking, red when something failed, violet when tracks wait for review. Cards that are all good look as before.
• Spotify now requires Premium for the apps people create, so Antiphon says so where you connect Spotify.
• The Spotify client ID guide follows Spotify's Create app form, with Copy buttons for the name, description and redirect URI. The client ID field has a Paste button, and Antiphon tells you if what you pasted isn't a client ID.

Focus for testers:
• Open the app: the seams being checked should show the spinning ring and the running border, which stop when the check is done.
• Sync a seam: its card should show the turning arrows and the gold border. A seam with tracks to review should have a violet border.
• Settings › Spotify › How to get one: follow the guide and use the Copy and Paste buttons.

What's new in 1.4.4:

• Fixed: a seam could show another seam's details, and its Sync now could preview the other seam. This happened after opening a seam from a sync's Review, a widget, Library or Search.
• Sync results you've already seen no longer come back later in the progress sheet. Sync now on a seam whose sync ended while hidden opens a fresh preview.

Focus for testers:
• Create two seams in a row (preview and sync each), then open the second one and tap Sync now: it should preview that seam.
• Tap Review on a sync's result, go back, open another seam: it should show that seam.

What's new in 1.4.3:

• A song you picked a version for now shows that version's cover in the seam's track list. The preview and the sync's progress sheet show the cover of the version being added.
• The sync's progress sheet no longer cuts off Pause, Hide or the note below them. It opens a little taller, and expanding it shows more of the tracklist.

Focus for testers:
• Pick another version for a song: its cover in the seam's list should change to the one you picked.
• Start a sync: Pause, Hide and "You can leave…" should be fully visible. Drag the sheet up: more tracks should appear.

What's new in 1.4.2:

• The bar above the tabs now opens only the sync's progress sheet, over whatever screen you're on. Before, it also brought back the preview behind it.
• Sync now on a seam that's already syncing shows its progress instead of a new preview.

Focus for testers:
• Start a sync, tap Hide, switch to another tab, then tap the bar above the tabs: only the progress sheet should open, on that tab.
• Start two syncs, hide them, tap the bar: switch between them with the covers at the top.

What's new in 1.4.1:

• Choose the version: tap any song in a seam, in Activity › New tracks, in a preview's track list or in Review, and pick exactly which version goes into the other playlist. The sheet says which seam it's for, the search starts with the song's artist and title, and your pick is kept. A synced song gets your pick in place of Antiphon's (in Apple Music playlists you made, Antiphon adds yours and tells you to delete the old one in Music). Undo puts the old version back.
• Album matching: "Same as source" now finds the same album even when Spotify and Apple Music name it differently ("Word Up! (The Remixes)" is "Word Up!: The Remixes - EP"), and "Original" no longer picks a later compilation.
• Fixed: previewing right after creating a new seam froze the app until iOS closed it.
• Fixed: hiding a running sync and reopening it from the bar above the tabs now shows the same tracklist and progress.
• Rows with a choice (and song rows) take a tap anywhere on the row.

Focus for testers:
• Create a new seam and preview it right away: it should load, not freeze.
• Start a sync, tap Hide, tap the bar above the tabs: same tracklist as before hiding.
• Pick another version for a synced song in one of your own Apple Music playlists: you should be told to delete the old one in Music.

What's new in 1.4.0:

• The sync sheet remembers what it was showing: hide it and reopen it (from the accessory, from anywhere in the app) and it picks up right where it left off, same tracklist and progress, without jumping you to a different screen.
• Several syncs at once now browse in one sheet: a strip of covers above the live view switches between them.
• An iPad pass: Library shows more than two covers at a time, and a few screens that used to stretch edge to edge (onboarding, the match evidence table, the close-match review card) now stay a readable width and center themselves.

Focus for testers on iPad specifically:
• Open Library, Settings, a sync preview and the close-match review screen (Syncs → a seam with "To review" → tap a track) and see if anything still looks stretched or oddly placed.
• Start two syncs at once (or start one, hide it, start a second), tap the accessory, and check that switching between them in the sheet shows the right tracklist for each.

What's new in 1.3.0:

• New design for iOS 26: Syncs, Library, Activity and Search on a Liquid Glass tab bar, and seams shown as two stitched covers.
• Every sync shows a preview first. Close matches wait in Review for your call, and removals follow each seam's rule (only add, mirror, or ask).
• Syncs keep running when you leave the app or lock the phone, with progress from iOS. Several seams can sync at once; tap the bar above the tabs to see them.
• Opening Antiphon checks every seam. Watched seams sync, and the others say what's waiting. Pull down on Syncs to check again right away.
• Activity lists every run, and most can be undone.
• Widgets: Seams, In sync (with Sync all), and New this week, plus a Live Activity while a sync runs.
• Answer questions from the notification: use a close match, skip it, or decide about a removal.
• Settings: monitoring interval, Wi-Fi only, quiet hours, removals in the background, appearance and app icon.

Focus for testers:
• Open the app after adding a track on Spotify: a watched seam should pick it up, and an unwatched seam's card should say "1 change waiting". Pull down to check again.
• Start a long sync, tap Hide, then switch apps or lock the phone. It should finish.
• Add the widgets and check that they match the app after syncing, linking or unlinking.
