# Antiphon support

Antiphon keeps playlists in step between Spotify and Apple Music, on your iPhone and iPad.

## Getting started

1. **Connect Spotify.** Antiphon uses a free Spotify developer client ID that you create. In the app, tap "How to get a Spotify client ID" and follow the five steps: open the Spotify developer dashboard, create an app, add the redirect URI shown in Antiphon, choose Web API, then copy the client ID into Antiphon.
2. **Connect Apple Music.** Allow access when iOS asks. Adding tracks to your library and playlists needs an Apple Music subscription.
3. **Make a seam.** Pick a playlist on one side; Antiphon finds or creates its twin, shows a preview of every change, and syncs when you say so.

## Common questions

**Why do I need my own Spotify client ID?** Antiphon has no server in between: it talks to Spotify directly as an app you own. That keeps your data between you, Spotify and Apple.

**A song is on one side but not the other.** Some songs aren't available on the other service in your country. Antiphon lists them as "Not available" instead of adding a wrong match.

**Antiphon asked me about a close match.** When it isn't sure two versions are the same recording (for example a remaster), it asks you in Review. Play both and choose.

**Can I undo a sync?** Yes. Open Activity, pick the sync and choose Undo.

**Does Antiphon remove songs?** Only if you turn that on for a seam. In a two-way seam it asks before removing anything.
